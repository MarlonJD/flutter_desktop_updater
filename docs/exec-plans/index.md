`active/` while they are being executed, move them to `completed/` after
verification, and record recurring cleanup work in `tech-debt-tracker.md`.

Plans must be self-contained, keep progress and decisions current, name exact
commands and observable evidence, and remain safe to resume without chat
history. A plan can describe Git checkpoints but does not grant commit, push,
branch, release, or external-write authority.
